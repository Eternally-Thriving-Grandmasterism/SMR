# 01 — Passive Shutdown Path (Design-Time)

**Checklist flag:** `passive_shutdown_path_documented`  
**Gate emphasis:** Compassion / Zero-Harm · Order  
**Contact:** info@Rathor.ai

> DESIGN-TIME ONLY. Conceptual safety argument structure — not certified protection-system design.

## Intent

Document a **passive** path to a safe state that does not rely on continuous operator action or powered active systems alone.

## Design-time requirements (to be expanded)

1. **Safe-state definition** — what “safe” means for thermal, reactivity, and containment proxies in this concept class.
2. **Driving forces** — gravity, natural circulation, material properties, or geometry that bias the system toward safe state.
3. **Independence** — which functions remain available if primary power and primary instrumentation are lost.
4. **Time margins** — qualitative discussion of grace time before irreversible harm thresholds (placeholders until physics models exist).
5. **Open audit** — arguments and assumptions published under AG-SML for independent scrutiny.

## Non-goals

- No claim of regulatory acceptance.
- No numerical trip setpoints presented as licensed limits.
- No substitution for site-specific engineering.

## Next engineering inputs (out of band)
