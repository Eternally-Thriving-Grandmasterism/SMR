# 06 — Local Operability Notes (Design-Time)

**Checklist flag:** `local_operability_notes`  
**Gate emphasis:** Service · Joy · Abundance  
**Contact:** info@Rathor.ai

> DESIGN-TIME ONLY. Operability intent for community-scale understanding — not an operations manual or training program.

## Intent

Keep the concept operable in principle by people and institutions that are not mega-capital operators alone: clear interfaces, modularity, and inspectability.

## Notes (initial)

1. **Module boundaries** — factory module vs site systems; clear mechanical/electrical/I&C seams.
2. **Inspection access** — passive features and sensors intended to be inspectable without heroic outages.
3. **Staffing philosophy** — reduce reliance on continuous high-intensity control actions (aligns with passive path).
4. **Language & docs** — safety and protocol docs remain human-readable (this repo).
5. **Local benefit** — heat/power product concepts should admit district or community coupling discussions without proprietary lock-in (AG-SML individual use).

## Non-goals

- Not a claim of simplified licensing.
- Not a staffing plan for any real plant.
